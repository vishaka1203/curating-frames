# Footer design refinement

References reviewed: House on the Clouds (https://www.houseontheclouds.com/), Erich McVey (https://www.erichmcvey.com/), and Jose Villa (https://www.josevilla.com/).

House on the Clouds separates social links and contact details across a quiet neutral footer. Erich McVey uses a restrained utility row with back-to-top, copyright, email and Instagram. Jose Villa makes phone and email explicit in its contact area. These informed hierarchy and simplicity; no branding, assets or exact layouts were copied.

Curating Frames retains the current ivory palette, wordmark, Instagram icon and enquiry headline. Its footer groups brand/social, navigation, and verified contact information on desktop. Mobile groups stack to avoid compressed email addresses, with a compact horizontal navigation row on smaller screens. Every footer link has a minimum 44px touch height. The utility row contains copyright, privacy and a native back-to-top anchor. No scrolling library or imagery changed.

Verified in-browser at 1280px desktop, 667px intermediate and 390px mobile: alignment, email wrapping, no horizontal overflow, accessible Instagram name and 44px link targets. Lint and strict typecheck pass.
